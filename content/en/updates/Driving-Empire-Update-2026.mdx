## What the Big Splash Update Added to Driving Empire

The **Big Splash update** is the centerpiece of the **Driving Empire update 2026** rollout, and it is the first time Voldex has added a full vehicle category through a real-world licensing deal rather than a generic Roblox reskin. The studio partnered with **Brunswick Corporation**, one of the largest recreational boating manufacturers in the United States, to bring four production-line boats into the game with accurate hull profiles, branded dashboards, and engine audio captured from the real watercraft.

According to the [official Driving Empire page on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/3351674303/Driving-Empire), the title is a licensed driving simulation with more than 200 real-world vehicles spanning drag racing, street circuits, ATV off-road, and now full boating gameplay. The Brunswick collaboration pushes that vehicle count past 210 and introduces a brand-new career path: water-based contracts, regatta-style races, and a new harbor hub that anchors the entire west side of the map.

### New Harbor Hub and Water-Based Game Modes

The map update shipped alongside the boats, expanding the coastline with a fully scripted **Harbor District** that includes fueling docks, a boat dealership, two regatta start gates, and a custom wake park. Three new water-based modes were added to the rotation:

- **Coastal Sprint** — point-to-point time trials along a buoy-marked route

- **Regatta Race** — 4-player circuit racing with staggered starts and clean-air rules

- **Trick Run** — a wake-based score attack where air time and rotation determine payout

The harbor also includes a working **fuel station** that drains in real time, meaning players who rage-quit a race in the middle of a leg will spawn back at the dealership with an empty tank. Community testers reported this was one of the more controversial design choices, because the walk back to the dock can stretch over a minute on a full server.


## March 2026 Patch Notes and Balance Changes

Beyond the headline Brunswick content, the **Driving Empire march 2026 update** also shipped a substantial balance pass aimed at evening out gaps that had crept into the meta since the 2025 winter patch. The changes touch every major discipline, and they are best understood as a bundle rather than a single list of buffs and nerfs.


### Drag Strip and Street Circuit Tuning

| Discipline | Change | Why It Matters |
|------------|--------|----------------|
| Drag Strip | **Tier-3 turbo** cooldown reduced by 1.2s | Lets mid-engine builds run a full pass without dumping NOS early |
| Drag Strip | **Nitrous refill** from sponsor crate cut from 40% to 25% | Forces commitment to a single big run per race |
| Street Circuit | **Downforce scaling** nerfed above 8.5/10 aero | Breaks the dominance of max-downforce GT builds on technical tracks |
| Street Circuit | **Brake bias** front limit raised to 70% | Rewards trail-brake players on hairpin-heavy circuits |

The drag changes ripple into the entire street economy, because nitro crates are a major trophy sink. Players who relied on stacking refills between rounds now have to weigh spending on a single max-effort run versus saving for the next race. According to community data posted in the official Discord after launch, average drag race payouts dropped by roughly 12% in the first week, which is consistent with players burning through fewer sponsored refills.


### Off-Road and ATV Trophy Economy

Off-road received the lightest touch, but the trophy economy shifted in a way that long-term grinders will notice. The **ATV off-road trophy multiplier** for three-star clean runs was raised from 1.5x to 1.8x, and dirt-road contracts now pay a flat 20,000 trophy bonus on completion regardless of vehicle. ATV players who used to skip contract chains in favor of free-roam laps now have a real reason to chase the contract board.

### Bug Fixes, Exploit Patches, and Anti-Cheat

The **Driving Empire patch notes** always include a bug-fix block, and the March 2026 list is unusually long. The headline items cover duplication exploits, physics desyncs, race-finish issues, and auto-farm scripts:

- Fixed a duplication exploit involving the **Benli Super Yacht** spawn menu that had been live since November 2025

- Resolved a physics desync on the **Highway Tunnel** street circuit that occasionally launched vehicles into the skybox

- Patched a race-finish desync that awarded trophies to players who had already been kicked from the server

- Disabled a third-party auto-farm script through a new **client-side integrity check** that flags inhuman shift-timing patterns

The exploit patches are the most consequential for the economy. Yacht duplication had been inflating the used-boat market on the trading plaza for months, and its removal is already showing up in the form of a 7-9% price recovery on legitimate listings, according to community-reported trade data from the first two weeks after the patch.


## Driving Empire Boats Update Guide: How to Get Started

If you are logging in for the first time since the Brunswick content went live, the new boating loop is structured so that almost anyone can reach the first competitive boat within a single play session. The progression has four steps, and they map cleanly onto the **Driving Empire boats update guide** flow that most content creators have been promoting.


### Step-by-Step Boating Progression

1. **Spawn at the Harbor District** — fast-travel is unlocked automatically after the first login post-patch.

2. **Pick up the free loaner boat** — a starter jet-boat is given to every player once per account.

3. **Complete the Coastal Sprint tutorial** — finishes in roughly four minutes and awards the **Coastal Rookie** trophy.

4. **Buy the Boston Whaler 170 Montauk** — the cheapest permanent Brunswick boat at roughly 380,000 in-game dollars.

After the Montauk, players typically grind regatta races for a few days before upgrading to the **Bayliner VR6 Bowrider** for sprint events, and eventually the **420 Outrage** for open-water races. The **Heyday WT Surf** sits slightly off the main progression line because it is built for the trick-score mode rather than head-to-head racing, so most players buy it last as a sidegrade.


### Tuning and Setup Tips for Brunswick Boats

Boat tuning is its own discipline, and it does not transfer directly from car tuning. According to community testing on the new Brunswick hulls, the most useful first-week adjustments are propeller pitch, hull balance, and throttle mapping:

- **Propeller pitch** is the marine equivalent of final drive — lower pitch equals better acceleration, higher pitch equals higher top speed.

- **Hull balance** shifts weight forward for tighter turns at the cost of top-end stability.

- **Throttle map** smooths the low-RPM range, which matters most on the Montauk because its real-world engine has a narrow power band.

A more detailed breakdown of how the new Brunswick boats compare against the meta of older car classes, including the impact of the downforce nerf on hybrid road-boat builds and the optimal propeller setups, is available in the [tuning and setup guide](/en/tuning/driving-empire-lap-time-tune-setup/).
