## The NASCAR Collaboration and Stock Car Update

The **NASCAR collaboration** is the centerpiece of mid-2026 content, marking the first time official stock cars enter the game. Players can unlock the **Toyota Camry NASCAR** through the NASCAR100M code and through event missions, while the **Betty Beater NASCAR** variant arrives via the BADGUYS2 code that ties into the parallel movie event. This is not a simple skin drop: the NASCAR update introduces dedicated stock car handling, draft mechanics on oval tracks, and a paint booth expansion that supports full custom liveries.

### How the NASCAR Content Drops

The collaboration runs in three layers. First, free codes drop the entry-level stock car. Second, the season pass includes premium NASCAR liveries and pit-crew cosmetics. Third, a new oval circuit joins the street and drag rotation, giving the stock cars a purpose-built track rather than bolting them onto an existing layout.

| NASCAR Reward | Unlock Path | Type | Difficulty to Obtain |
|---------------|-------------|------|----------------------|
| Toyota Camry NASCAR | NASCAR100M code | Stock car | Easy |
| Betty Beater NASCAR | BADGUYS2 code | Movie-tied stock car | Easy |
| Premium NASCAR liveries | Season Pass tier 30+ | Cosmetic | Medium |
| Oval circuit access | Free event milestone | Track | Free |

The code-based unlocks reward active players who track seasonal drops, while the season pass path rewards consistent play. According to community reports, draft mechanics make pack racing viable on the oval, which is a noticeable change from the solo time-trial focus of drag mode. The collaboration signals that Voldex is investing in team-style formats, an angle that may expand into convoy events later in the year.

The **Bad Guys 2 collaboration** lands as the first movie tie-in event of 2026, themed around the animated sequel. The event layers a short heist mission structure over existing race modes, and rewards are tied to the same code distribution that the NASCAR content uses. Players who redeem BADGUYS2 receive the **Betty Beater NASCAR**, a vehicle that bridges the two crossovers by combining the stock car format from NASCAR with the movie's character art.

## Season Pass Update and Progression Rework

The **season pass update** replaces the previous event-by-event reward structure with a unified tier system that spans the entire 2026 cycle. Every event, code drop, and collaboration feeds XP into the same pass, which removes the frustration of missing out on a single limited-time tier and replaces it with catch-up XP multipliers.

### Pass Structure and Tier Economics

The new pass introduces 100 tiers per season, with both a free track and a premium track running in parallel. Premium tiers cost Robux but include exclusive cars, liveries, and customization items. Free tiers include cash, trophies, and a rotating selection of customization kits. The change is significant because the previous structure made every event feel mandatory, while the unified pass gives casual players a way to progress without chasing every drop.

| Pass Type | Tier Range | Cost | Reward Type |
|-----------|------------|------|-------------|
| Free pass | All 100 tiers | None | Cash, kits, trophies |
| Premium pass | All 100 tiers | Robux | Exclusive cars, liveries |
| Event XP boost | Tiers 1-20 | Earned | 2x XP during active events |
| Catch-up XP | Tiers 80-100 | Earned | 1.5x XP after week 8 |

The rework also introduces a catch-up mechanic in the final 20 tiers, which is a community-requested feature. Players who skip early weeks can still close the gap through boosted XP, which softens the FOMO pressure that defined the 2025 event cadence. The premium track includes the NASCAR liveries and the Bad Guys 2 movie cosmetics, which is the main reason the pass is positioned as a value bundle rather than a pay-to-skip shortcut.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the biggest Driving Empire new features in 2026?**

The biggest additions are the NASCAR stock car collaboration, the Bad Guys 2 movie tie-in event, the unified season pass progression system, and the Brunswick boating expansion. Each feature connects to the others through the season pass, so players who engage with one crossover also progress toward rewards in the others.

**How do I unlock the Betty Beater NASCAR from the Bad Guys 2 collaboration?**

You unlock the **Betty Beater NASCAR** by redeeming the BADGUYS2 code through the gear icon in the settings menu. The same vehicle also appears as a finale reward in the Bad Guys 2 event missions, so players who miss the code can still earn it through gameplay.

**Is the Driving Empire season pass worth the Robux cost?**

The premium pass is worth it for players who log in regularly, because the NASCAR liveries, Bad Guys 2 cosmetics, and exclusive cars cover the tier cost within a few weeks of active play. Casual players who only check in during major events get more value from the free track and the code-based unlocks.

**What is on the future cars roadmap for late 2026?**

The confirmed roadmap includes holiday event cars for Halloween and winter, plus additional licensed partnerships that have not been announced. According to community reports, the Q4 2026 cycle will feature at least one more major crossover alongside the seasonal content, keeping the car list expansion on a quarterly cadence.

**Do NASCAR and Bad Guys 2 events overlap or run separately?**

The two events run in parallel during the mid-2026 window, and the codes for each collaboration are live simultaneously. Players can progress through both tracks at the same time, and the season pass unifies the XP gains so that progress in one event feeds the other.
