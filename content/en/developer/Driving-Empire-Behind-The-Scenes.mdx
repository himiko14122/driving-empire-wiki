## Voldex, the Studio Powering Driving Empire

The most important fact about Driving Empire behind the scenes is that the game is not a solo project. According to the [official Driving Empire page on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/3351674303/Driving-Empire), the title is built and operated by Voldex, a studio that has built a reputation for licensed driving simulations. The studio handles licensing, physics, vehicle modeling, and live operations, and the result is a game with 200+ real-world vehicles spanning Lamborghini, Porsche, Ferrari, Nissan, Toyota, Chevrolet, and Ford.

Voldex's behind-the-scenes structure shows up in three ways that players actually feel in-game, each tied to a specific role rather than a vague "the studio handles it." The licensing pipeline is why a Boston Whaler boat shows up next to a Porsche 911 in the same Build-A-Car event, the live-ops cadence is why codes drop on a predictable weekly rhythm instead of randomly, and the cross-channel publishing is why the same Driving Empire content creator spotlight appears on YouTube, TikTok, and X within the same 24-hour window.

- **Licensing pipeline**: Vehicles arrive through real OEM partnerships, which is why the [March 2026 Big Splash update](/en/updates/driving-empire-update-2026/) added four Boston Whaler, Bayliner, and Heyday boats from Brunswick Corporation. Brunswick's marketing VP Jamie Evans and Voldex licensing manager Stephen Wanigesekera both appear in the announcement cycle, which is unusual transparency for a Roblox title.

- **Live-ops cadence**: Codes, Build-A-Car events, and seasonal drops follow a steady rhythm because the studio plans them around partnerships, not just community polls.

- **Cross-channel publishing**: The same team that pushes Roblox updates also posts on YouTube, TikTok, and X, which is what makes the content creator spotlight feel integrated rather than outsourced.

| Behind-the-Scenes Role | What They Actually Do | Player-Facing Output |
|---|---|---|
| Voldex dev team | Physics, UI, anti-cheat | Patches, hotfixes, server stability |
| Licensing manager | OEM deals (Brunswick, Porsche, etc.) | New vehicles, Build-A-Car events |
| Community managers | Discord moderation, bug triage | Patch notes, FAQ replies, event timing |
| Content creator program | YouTube/TikTok partnerships | Spotlight videos, code drops |
| Merch pipeline | Apparel, accessories, collabs | Limited drops, seasonal bundles |

The takeaway: when you see a new boat type or a limited Porsche drop, there is a real person on the Voldex side whose job is to make that happen, and the player community is the audience they are optimizing for.
