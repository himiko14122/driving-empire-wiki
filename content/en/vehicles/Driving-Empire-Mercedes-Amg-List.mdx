### How the AMG Tiers Map to Driving Empire Gameplay

The **Entry** and **Mid** AMGs (GLB 35, C 63 S, CLA 45 S, GLA 45 S) are the gateway tier: cheap enough to unlock within a single play session, but with grip and top speed that are perfectly tuned for street circuit and drag beginners. Players running these cars in drag strips still post competitive 0-60 times because the lighter curb weight keeps launch acceleration sharp, even when the horsepower figure looks modest next to a 720 HP Black Series.

The **Mid-High** and **High** tier (E 63 S, GLC 63 S, GLE 63 S, GLS 63, AMG GT, AMG GT R) is where AMG starts competing with European exotics. The E 63 S in particular is a favorite for street circuit because the AWD system lets you trail-brake into corners without snapping oversteer, while the AMG GT R rewards experienced players who know how to manage RWD throttle on corner exit. The **Top** tier holds only the AMG GT Black Series, which trades daily-driver practicality for a 720 HP engine that is, in community testing, within striking distance of McLaren and Bugatti hypercars in straight-line speed.

## Strategies for Building a Mercedes-Focused Garage

A Mercedes-focused garage works best when you progress through the AMG tiers in roughly the order they were released into the game. The **GLB 35** is the cheapest AMG and should be your first Mercedes — it costs around 135,000 cash and can be earned within a few races. The **CLA 45 S** or **C 63 S** comes next, at 145,000-160,000, and offers noticeably better grip on street circuits. From there, the **GLC 63 S** and **E 63 S** form the natural bridge into the high tier because both add AWD, which dramatically improves drag launch consistency.

The mid-to-high AMGs (GLE 63 S, GLS 63, AMG GT) are where you start competing with McLaren 570S and Chevrolet Corvette C8 owners. The **AMG GT R** is the last meaningful upgrade before the Black Series, and players who max out the GT R's transmission and tire tuning can sometimes beat stock Black Series builds in drag events, which is why the GT R has a loyal community following. The **AMG GT Black Series** is the endgame Mercedes and the only AMG with verified top-tier 720 HP output.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

The most common mistake AMG buyers make is upgrading to the AMG GT Black Series before they have the transmission and tire parts to support it. A stock Black Series is fast, but a tuned AMG GT R will beat a stock Black Series in most events. Another frequent error is assuming the GLS 63 (the largest SUV) is the best drag AMG — it is not, because the curb weight offsets the AWD advantage. Finally, players who already own the Chevrolet Corvette Z06 sometimes skip straight to the Black Series; this is fine for endgame, but the Z06 is a far better mid-game car than the GLE 63 S, so prioritize the Corvette during the mid tier and add the Black Series once your garage has stabilized.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many Mercedes-AMG cars are in Driving Empire?**

The community-tracked Driving Empire Mercedes-AMG list currently contains eleven models, ranging from the 225 HP GLB 35 entry SUV to the 720 HP AMG GT Black Series top-tier sports car. Voldex adds new AMG trims during seasonal updates, so the count may grow after each major patch.

**What is the best Mercedes-AMG for drag racing?**

The **AMG GT Black Series** is the best Mercedes drag car because its 720 HP and RWD layout reward perfect launch timing on the quarter-mile. For mid-game players, the **E 63 S** offers AWD launches that are easier to master and remain competitive in daily drag lobbies.

**Is the Mercedes-AMG GT Black Series faster than the Bugatti Chiron?**

The Black Series matches the Chiron in raw horsepower (720 HP) but trails in top speed (215 mph vs 218 mph) and AWD launch grip. The Chiron remains the drag king, while the Black Series is the better all-around pick for players who also race street circuits and event lobbies.

**How do I unlock the Betty Beater from Bad Guys 2?**

Enter the **BADGUYS2** code through the gear icon in settings, which grants the Betty Beater NASCAR stock car as a redeemable reward. The code is part of the Bad Guys 2 movie tie-in collaboration and is event-locked, so it may expire after the collaboration window closes.

**Should I buy a Chevrolet Corvette Z06 or save for an AMG GT Black Series?**

Buy the Z06 first if your garage budget is under 500,000 in-game cash, because the Z06 is the best mid-tier step before hypercars. The Black Series is worth saving for once you have 800,000+ cash and the transmission/tire parts to tune it, otherwise the AMG GT R is a strong placeholder.
