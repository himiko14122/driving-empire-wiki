The Driving Empire Fairway Zoomer is one of the strangest free-wheelers you can pull out of a code, and pairing it with the Renault Twizy, the Benli yacht, and the new Brunswick boats makes for a genuinely weird garage. This guide lines up the **Driving Empire Fairway Zoomer** against its closest oddball rivals so you can decide whether the golf-cart lifestyle, the city quadricycle, or the brand-new Brunswick water fleet actually fits your driving style.

## The Driving Empire Fairway Zoomer at a Glance

The Fairway Zoomer arrived as a 2023-model reward tied to the **ZOOM** code on the [Driving Empire codes list](/en/codes/driving-empire-codes-today/), and it is a licensed recreation of a real-world **Fairway Zoomer** neighborhood electric vehicle rather than a tuned sports car. Because it sits in the "lifestyle EV" niche, its numbers skew low compared with any supercar, but it lands surprisingly high on the fun-per-credit scale. According to the [official Driving Empire Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/3351674303/Driving-Empire), the game lists 200+ licensed vehicles, and the Zoomer is one of only a handful that genuinely look out of place at a drag strip.

### What the Golf Cart Is Built For

The Zoomer is a four-seat, open-frame, low-power people mover, so it behaves like a glorified golf cart in a server full of Lamborghinis. Community testing reported by players notes a top speed in the **55-65 mph** range, which is slow by drag-strip standards but more than enough for cruising the main avenues, the dealership lot, and the residential corners of the map. The torque curve is gentle, the handling is light, and the turning radius is the tightest of any car in this price bracket, which makes it a stealth pick for tight city blocks.

### Why the Zoomer Feels Different

Three quirks define the experience. First, the seating position is upright, so you get a near-pedestrian view of traffic. Second, the suspension is bouncy over curb cuts, and you can feel the body roll in long sweepers because the wheelbase is so short. Third, because it tops out early, you spend more time in third and fourth gear than in fifth, which trains a very different shifting rhythm than a tuned GT-R. None of this is a downside; it just means the Zoomer plays a different minigame than the rest of your garage.

### Where the Zoomer Sits in the Lifestyle EV Niche

If you sort the licensed vehicles by category, the Zoomer sits next to a very small group that also includes the Renault Twizy. The Twizy is a two-seat urban quadricycle, so it has a tighter cabin but a slightly more car-like silhouette. In a pure personality contest, the Twizy feels like a city commuter, while the Zoomer feels like a resort shuttle. Neither is going to win you a drag race, but both are the only vehicles in the game where you can pull up next to a Bugatti and have the entire server laugh at the visual contrast.

## Driving Empire Renault Twizy vs Fairway Zoomer

The Renault Twizy is the second most obvious comparison point, and it shares the same "lifestyle EV" DNA as the Zoomer. Both are slow, both are short, and both are wildly impractical for serious racing. The differences show up in the way they handle, in the seat count, and in the kind of content each one is best suited for.

## Driving Empire Benli Yacht Stats and Why the Zoomer Is the Bizarre Counterpart

Pulling the **Driving Empire Benli yacht stats** into the conversation feels almost absurd, but the Benli is the only other "lifestyle reward" in the game that ignores racing entirely, just like the Zoomer. The Benli is a super-yacht, so it lives on the boating map and behaves like a floating status symbol, whereas the Zoomer lives on the road and behaves like a rolling status symbol. They share the same design philosophy: low performance, high visual bragging rights.

### Benli Yacht Snapshot

The Benli pre-dates the Brunswick partnership, which is why it was the only watercraft in Driving Empire before the **Big Splash update** rolled out. According to the March 2026 update notes, the Benli was the flagship water vehicle and the standard by which Brunswick licensing manager Stephen Wanigesekera benchmarked the new boats. It is large, expensive, and dramatically slower than any of the Brunswick boats in a straight line, but it has the largest interior, the most seating, and the highest cosmetic ceiling of anything that floats in the game.

## The Brunswick Big Splash Boats vs the Zoomer Mentality

The Brunswick Big Splash update added four new licensed watercraft to Driving Empire, which is important context for the Zoomer because it changes the "lifestyle" reward pool. Before Big Splash, the only lifestyle option in the water was the Benli, and on land the only true lifestyle option was the Zoomer. After Big Splash, there are now five lifestyle options total, and three of them are boats.

### Driving Empire Boston Whaler 170 Montauk

The **Driving Empire Boston Whaler 170 Montauk** is the most balanced of the four Brunswick boats. It has a moderate top speed, a forgiving hull, and enough deck space to ferry a full four-player crew across the boating map. According to the Big Splash update notes, the Boston Whaler 170 Montauk was positioned as the everyday pick, the one that you can race without feeling undergunned but also cruise around the bay without feeling like you are overspec'd.

### Driving Empire Bayliner VR6 Bowrider

The **Driving Empire Bayliner VR6 Bowrider** is the speedboat of the pack. It has the highest top speed among the Brunswick boats and a sharp turn-in, which makes it the natural pick for water races. Community testing reported by players suggests the Bayliner VR6 Bowrider pulls ahead of the Boston Whaler 170 Montauk in straight lines, but the Montauk holds tighter in chicanes because of its wider beam. If you are coming from a road racing background, the Bayliner is the most intuitive boat in the lineup.

### Driving Empire Heyday WT Surf

The **Driving Empire Heyday WT Surf** is the wake-sports specialist. It is wider than the other Brunswick boats, so it corners flat, and it produces a heavier wake that pushes other players around when you cut across their line. The Heyday WT Surf is not the fastest boat in the game, but it is the most fun boat in the game if you treat it as a wake-sports toy rather than a racing boat. Think of it as the water equivalent of the Fairway Zoomer: not fast, very expressive, and a magnet for social play.

## When to Pick the Zoomer Over Anything Else

The Zoomer is the right pick in a small but very specific set of situations, and the wrong pick almost everywhere else. If you are grinding trophies in the oval, picking a NASCAR or a tuned muscle car is the obvious move. If you are grinding a Build-A-Car event, you want a car that can collect parts fast, which usually means a tuned coupe. The Zoomer is the right pick when the goal is to be seen, not to be fast.

### Situations Where the Zoomer Shines

- Cruising the dealership strip with a full four-player crew, because no other car in the game seats four lifestyle passengers.

- Filming social content, because the silhouette is unique enough to read instantly.

- Running casual depot-to-depot shuttle runs, where the tight turning circle matters more than the top speed.

- Pairing with the **HAPPY2026 Renault Twizy** for a two-car lifestyle convoy, which is one of the rare build themes in Driving Empire.

- Driving into a server full of supercars and watching the chat react to the visual whiplash.

### Situations Where the Zoomer Struggles

- Drag strip events, where the top speed gap is brutal.

- Long highway cruises, where you will get lapped by every tuned supercar in the server.

- Off-road ATV events, because the Zoomer is not a four-wheel-drive vehicle.

- Water races, where the Zoomer is, of course, completely absent and you would want a Brunswick boat instead.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I unlock the Driving Empire Fairway Zoomer?**

Enter the code **ZOOM** in the gear icon under the in-game settings menu, then restart your session if the vehicle does not appear in your garage. The reward is the 2023 Fairway Zoomer and is permanent once claimed, so you only need to redeem it once per account.

**Is the Fairway Zoomer faster than the Renault Twizy?**

No. Community testing on the Driving Empire beta branches suggests the Renault Twizy has a marginally higher top speed than the Fairway Zoomer, although the Zoomer is easier to handle in tight corners and seats twice as many players. Both are lifestyle EVs, not racing tools, so neither is going to win a drag race against a tuned supercar.

**Which Brunswick boat should I buy first, the Boston Whaler 170 Montauk or the Bayliner VR6 Bowrider?**

Buy the Boston Whaler 170 Montauk first if you are learning the boating map, because it is more forgiving in turns and ferries a full crew. Buy the Bayliner VR6 Bowrider first if you are already comfortable with water racing and want the highest top speed among the Brunswick boats.

**Do I need the Benli yacht if I already have the Brunswick boats?**

You do not need it, but the Benli is still the largest interior boat in the game and the only one that pre-dates the Brunswick partnership. If you care about cosmetics and server presence, the Benli is a status piece. If you care about racing, the Bayliner VR6 Bowrider is a better pick and is also cheaper.

**Can the Driving Empire Fairway Zoomer win any event?**

Practically no on a straight-line event, but yes in any event that scores on social play, style, or four-person crew presence. The Zoomer is also the tightest-turning four-seat vehicle in Driving Empire, so it can win very specific obstacle-style challenges where agility matters more than top speed.
