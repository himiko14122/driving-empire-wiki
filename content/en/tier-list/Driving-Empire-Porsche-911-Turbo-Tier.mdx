## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the Porsche 911 Turbo S tier in Driving Empire?**

No, the 911 Turbo is firmly A tier in the June 2026 meta. According to community testing, the Corvette Z06R holds the S-tier slot in street circuit because of its better high-speed cornering and lower tire wear. The 911 Turbo gets close but loses in 60% of head-to-head races against a tuned Z06R.

**Should I pick the 911 Turbo or the Camaro ZL1 first?**

Pick the ZL1 if you have the $4.5M dealership buy, because it wins drag and still places top-three in street circuit. Pick the 911 Turbo if you already own the ZL1 or you primarily race technical circuits. The 911 Turbo is also event-locked, so if the 50th anniversary event is running, grab the parts before grinding for a ZL1.

**What is the best car for each mode in Driving Empire right now?**

The ZL1 (or tuned R35) wins drag, the 911 Turbo or Z06R wins street circuit, dedicated off-road builds win ATV, and the Boston Whaler 170 is the meta pick for boating after the March 2026 Brunswick Big Splash update. No single car is meta in all four modes.

**Are there any cheap but fast cars that rival the 911 Turbo?**

Yes — the Nissan GT-R (R35) is free via the 2MLIKES code and performs within 3% of a tuned 911 Turbo on most street circuits. The Toyota Supra MK4 and Mazda RX-7 FD are also strong sub-$1M picks that, with the right tuning, can challenge A-tier rivals in head-to-head races.

**How do I tune the 911 Turbo for street circuit races?**

Focus the build on **braking balance**, **downforce**, and **gear ratio compression**. The 911 Turbo's main advantage is mid-corner grip, so prioritize the handling and braking stats over raw top speed. A properly tuned 911 Turbo is roughly 1.5 seconds faster per lap than a stock build on the standard street circuit.
