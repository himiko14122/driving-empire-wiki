## What "Free Car" Means in Driving Empire

Before the tiering makes sense, the term "free car" needs a precise definition. In Driving Empire, three distinct paths can put a vehicle in your garage without opening your wallet, and each path has different expectations for availability and effort.

The first and most common source is **active code drops**. Developer Voldex publishes codes on the official Roblox game page, the Driving Empire Discord, and the X/Twitter account. Codes typically expire within two to four weeks of release, and the rewards rotate between cash payouts, Trophies, Customization Kits, and actual vehicle unlocks. Recent confirmed drops per community reports include 2MLIKES (2017 Nissan GT-R), MARCH2026 (2012 Chevrolet Camaro ZL1), CALL911 (50,911 cash), SPOOKY (80,000 cash), BADGUYS2 (Betty Beater NASCAR), NASCAR100M (200 Trophies), ZOOM (2023 Fairway Zoomer), CUSTOMIZATION2025 (10 Kits), and 10KITS (10 Customization Kits).

The second source is **event handouts**. Limited-time seasonal events and milestone celebrations frequently grant a free vehicle to every participant, often gated by a questline or a login streak. The 2023 Porsche 911 Turbo 50th Anniversary event, for instance, asked players to collect scattered parts across the map to unlock an exclusive Porsche variant.

The third path is **starter cars**: the vehicles every new account receives or can buy near-instantly with starting cash. These sit at the bottom of the power curve but remain the foundation of any free-to-play progression plan.

## T0 Free Cars: Code Drops and Event Crown Jewels

T0 in a free tier list is a stricter label than T0 in a paid tier list. A paid T0 is a car that outperforms 95% of the roster. A free T0 only needs to outperform every other free option and stay competitive with mid-tier paid cars in its best discipline. With that bar set, the four T0 picks earn their place through measured test data, player consensus, and consistent dominance in their event metas.


### 2017 Nissan GT-R (Code: 2MLIKES)

The 2MLIKES code has remained one of the most valuable handouts in the game's history because the 2017 Nissan GT-R is a genuine drag weapon. Community-reported quarter-mile times put it within striking distance of paid R35 variants, and the launch traction holds on both the drag strip and the street circuit. Players who entered 2MLIKES during its active window secured a T0-tier drag car for the cost of a single redemption, and it remains the headline example of a free car outperforming its price tag.

Under repeated street-circuit heat cycling, the GT-R's stock rotors fade faster than those on heavier T0 exotics like the Regera, letting cars such as the free McLaren P1 close the gap through late-braking zones where straight-line advantage is irrelevant. The free-to-paid performance delta therefore hinges on track type: on straight drag strips and long highways, the GT-R still posts the best free-car ETs, but multi-corner circuits demand either a paid hypercar or a rotor upgrade. This brake-fade behavior is the main reason the 2MLIKES GT-R sits at the top of the drag tier list but only mid-pack in circuit tier rankings.


### 2012 Chevrolet Camaro ZL1 (Code: MARCH2026)

The MARCH2026 code arrived as part of the Big Splash launch window in March 2026 and delivered the 2012 Chevrolet Camaro ZL1. This generation of Camaro ZL1 is widely considered the strongest American muscle car in the free roster, with launch numbers that challenge the GT-R and a chassis tune that handles street-circuit hairpins better than the Nissan.

For American-muscle fans, MARCH2026 was the most important code drop in years, because it placed a genuinely competitive Chevy in the free pool at a time when muscle cars are surging in the meta. Treat the ZL1 as a T0 street and drag car and you will not be disappointed.


### 2023 Fairway Zoomer (Code: ZOOM)

The ZOOM code rewarded players with the 2023 Fairway Zoomer, a newer-model ATV that has rewritten expectations for the off-road discipline. According to community testing, the Zoomer finishes the ATV dirt loop faster than several paid ATV options, which is a remarkable result for a code-rewarded vehicle.

For players whose **driving empire free car tier ranking** revolves around the ATV dirt loop, the 2023 Fairway Zoomer is essentially the strongest free code reward available and should be redeemed before any other ZOOM-adjacent claim. Drag and street racers can still slot it into their rotation as a backup off-road option, but on technical grass and dirt turns it noticeably outpaces the Stingray and older Rhino-class ATVs, making it an S-tier pickup for off-road mains and a comfortable A-tier sidecar for everyone else.


### Boston Whaler 170 Montauk (Big Splash Update Bonus)

The March 2026 Big Splash update, a partnership between Voldex and Brunswick Corporation, added multiple new boats to the roster and the Boston Whaler 170 Montauk was the standout free handout. Players who logged in during the launch window received the Montauk as a login bonus, and the boat's handling on the new water-physics system has earned it a spot in the free T0 band alongside the four-wheeled code drops.

## T1 Free Cars: Solid Workhorses Worth Claiming

T1 in the free tier list is the workhorse band. These are cars that do not win outright in their best discipline but hold up across multiple race modes, or they specialize hard enough to earn a starting slot. Most active free-to-play players will end up with two or three T1 cars in their garage once codes and events dry up.


### Betty Beater NASCAR (Code: BADGUYS2)

The BADGUYS2 code unlocks the Betty Beater NASCAR, a stock-car variant that excels on long street circuits and banked ovals. Its top speed is excellent, but the launch is slow compared to the GT-R and Camaro ZL1, which keeps it out of T0.

Street-circuit and NASCAR-mode lobby hosts should prioritize redeeming BADGUYS2 early, since Betty Beater's high top speed shines on long straights and banked turns where launch deficits matter less, while pure drag racers—who accelerate only in short bursts and never hit the car's optimal speed band—gain almost nothing from adding it to their T1 rotation.


### Porsche 911 Turbo 50th Anniversary (2023 Build-A-Car Event)

The Porsche 911 Turbo 50th Anniversary was the headline reward of the 2023 Build-A-Car event, which required players to collect limited-time parts scattered across the map. Players who completed the event unlocked an exclusive Porsche variant that remains one of the best-looking free cars in the game and holds a solid T1 spot in drag and street performance.

Players who missed the 2023 Build-A-Car event currently cannot re-obtain the Porsche 911 Turbo 50th Anniversary through normal gameplay, making it effectively a T1 locked collectible. Voldex has hinted at returning Build-A-Car formats for future milestones, so tracking official Roblox announcements and the driving empire community car votes feed is the best way to catch the next rerun before parts disappear from the map again.


### Bayliner VR6 Bowrider (Big Splash Event)

The Big Splash update's water expansion also distributed the Bayliner VR6 Bowrider as an event handout. The Bayliner sits a tier below the Boston Whaler in pure handling but outperforms the older Benli super yacht on speed, which makes it a versatile T1 boating pick for players who missed the Montauk login window.


### Legacy 2017 Chevrolet Camaro ZL1 (Older Code Drops)

Earlier code handouts distributed a 2017 Chevrolet Camaro ZL1, which was a strong free pick at the time of release. As the 2026 meta has evolved, the 2017 ZL1 has been outclassed by the MARCH2026 ZL1, dropping it from T0 to T1. Players who already own it can still use it confidently, but new players should not chase the older code path.


## T2 Free Cars: Situational Picks and Starter Vehicles

T2 is where the free roster starts to disappoint. These cars are not bad, they are simply outclassed by the T0 and T1 options in nearly every scenario. Most T2 vehicles still earn a place in your early garage because you need at least one car to start the campaign, but the goal is to replace them as quickly as possible.


### Default Starter Sedan

Every new Driving Empire account begins with a starter sedan that handles poorly in all four race modes. The starter is intentionally weak to give new players a sense of progression when they upgrade, and it should be replaced within the first hour of play with either a starter cash purchase or a redeemed code car.

The fastest upgrade path is to enter all currently active codes immediately, then use the cash payouts to buy a cheap paid car as a stopgap. The starter sedan should never be your primary vehicle past your first five races.


### Free Trial Exotic (Rotating)

The game occasionally offers a free trial exotic through daily login rewards or promotional events. These trial vehicles are usually competent drag cars but locked to a single discipline, which keeps them in T2. Use them to test whether a paid exotic is worth grinding for, then move on.
