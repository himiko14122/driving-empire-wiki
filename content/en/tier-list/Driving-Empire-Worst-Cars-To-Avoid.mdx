## How to Spot a Rip-Off Before You Buy It

The dealership floor rotates every few weeks, which means new trap cars land in the showroom regularly. ### Five Red Flags on the Spec Card

- **0–60 time above 3.2 seconds on a "hypercar" badge** — that car is geared for top speed, not drag, and it will lose every launch.

- **Cornering grip under 0.85g** — uncompetitive in any circuit race, even with tire upgrades.

- **Tuning slot count under 4** — no room for turbo, brakes, and tires together, so the car is locked out of meta builds.

- **Resale value under 50%** — the car is marked down because community data shows it underperforms, not because of a sale event.

- **No Build-A-Car event support** — limited-time parts cannot be applied, so the car never gets event-exclusive upgrades.

Players who run through this checklist before every purchase cut their bad-buy rate dramatically, and they end up with garages full of S-tier cars instead of showpieces that never leave the lot.
