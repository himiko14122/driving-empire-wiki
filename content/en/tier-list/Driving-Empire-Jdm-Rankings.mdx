### How Driving Empire Measures Performance

The game tracks three core stats per vehicle: top speed, launch acceleration (which decides drag races), and grip (which decides circuit races). Quarter-mile times are the most common comparison metric, and the top JDM cars all clear the run in under 8 seconds once tuned. The grip stat matters most on the Street Circuit and the newer Brunswick boating crossovers, where weight transfer and braking zones decide positions. Off-road events devalue low ground clearance, which is why the JDM sports car segment is not universal — SUVs and trucks do most of the heavy lifting on the ATV dirt tracks.
